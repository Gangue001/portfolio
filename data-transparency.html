Data Transparency for AP News

UX/UI redesign · March – April 2026

I redesigned AP News’s cookie consent flow so readers could accept, reject, or customize their preferences when they first arrived, and revisit those choices later.

Figma prototype ↗

Design process / AP News

Redesigning cookie consent

I looked at where AP News surfaced privacy controls, how its policy explained data use, and how other sites presented consent. I used those findings to design a clearer flow from first visit to saved preferences.

01

Finding the privacy controls

When I mapped the existing flow, I found the main privacy links in the footer. Some tracking preferences could be adjusted with toggles, while other requests required a separate form. The privacy policy described these options in detail, but the dense text made them difficult to find quickly.

02

Sketching the first visit

After comparing consent banners and preference centers, I sketched three actions for the first visit: Accept All, Reject All, and Manage Choices. I also mapped where to confirm the choice and how readers could reopen settings later.

03

Organizing the preference center

In the wireframes, I grouped settings into necessary, analytics, personalization, and advertising cookies. The final prototype uses expandable sections so readers can scan the categories and open the ones they want to understand or adjust.

04

Confirming and revisiting choices

The Figma prototype shows a confirmation after preferences are saved. A persistent cookie icon reopens the settings, and a link leads to the full privacy policy for readers who want more detail.
