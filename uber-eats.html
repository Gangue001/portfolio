Uber Eats One-Handed Interaction Redesign

Interaction design & usability testing · November – December 2025

I designed a one-handed ordering flow that keeps key controls within thumb reach. A circular dial helps users browse categories, while larger quantity buttons and a shortcut menu support ordering and checkout.

In paper prototype sessions with five participants, some missed the long-press shortcut to the cart, and others found the dial easy to mistap. The sessions pointed to clearer gesture cues and larger touch targets.
